PIVOT ASCENT - APP REVIEW NOTES

PIVOT ASCENT is a portrait puzzle game from Decent Games. The player taps to swing between pivot nodes, collects gems, avoids hazards, and reaches the summit.

How to review
1. Open the app and choose PLAY.
2. Select any unlocked level. Progression starts at Level 1.
3. Tap the screen to control the swing and reach the green summit marker.
4. Return to the home screen to try DAILY CHALLENGE.

Account and access
No sign-in, account, subscription, purchase, or special hardware is required.

Network behavior
The game is designed to remain playable without a network connection. Confirm the final build behavior after any third-party SDKs are added.
